Why I'm Interested in the Human Eye

I am really interested in the human eye because it is amazing how such a small organ lets us see the entire world around us. It fascinates me how all the different parts, like the cornea, lens, and retina, work together to take in light and send signals to the brain to create a clear picture. I also find it cool that our eyes can show emotion and help us connect with other people without even saying a word Studying the eye feels like the perfect mix of science and everyday life, which makes me want to learn more about it.

My Future Dream: Becoming an Optometrist

I want to become an optometrist because I know people who work in this career. I have had the opportunity to see them work, and that made me even more interested in becoming an optometrist. I hope to work hard, learn new things, and achieve my dream of becoming an optometrist one day!
